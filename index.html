What is UniNav

Navigation built
for campus life

A large university campus can be disorienting — especially in the first weeks. Manipal UniNav was built to change that: an interactive navigation platform that makes finding your way across MUJ effortless, for anyone.

Powered by OpenStreetMap and the OpenRouteService API, UniNav renders accurate walking directions across every corner of the campus — entirely free and open source.

The campus is split into two navigation zones so routing stays clean and focused, whether your destination is a lecture hall or the mess.

I

College Campus

Academic blocks, libraries, laboratories, administrative buildings, and every facility across the main teaching campus.

II

Hostel Campus

Student residences, mess areas, recreational zones, and all destinations within the residential precinct.

III

Open Source

Both modules are open and freely deployable — lightweight, fast, and maintained as two separate repositories.

Getting started

How UniNav works

01

Open the website

Visit manipalmap.vercel.app in any browser. The interactive MUJ campus map loads immediately — no app, no install.

02

Search a location

Type any building, block, or facility — Library, Hostel B, Mess Hall — into the search bar at the top of the map.

03

Get directions

Tap the pin to open location details and request walking directions. The route is highlighted across the map instantly.

04

Navigate freely

Follow the path to your destination. The map updates as you move through any part of the MUJ campus.
