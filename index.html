What is UniNav

Open-source navigation
for a smarter campus

Finding your way across Manipal University Jaipur shouldn't require asking three people for directions. UniNav is a civic-grade interactive map platform built from the ground up for the MUJ community.

Built on OpenStreetMap open geographic data and real-time routing from OpenRouteService (ORS), the platform delivers accurate walking directions across the entire campus — free to access, forever.

The project runs as two independently deployed modules, each serving a distinct zone of campus life — academic and residential.

MAP OpenStreetMap — open geographic data
ROUTE OpenRouteService API — walking directions
DEPLOY Vercel — global edge deployment
PWA Service Worker — offline-ready

College Campus Zone

Getting started

How UniNav works

STEP 01

Open the platform

Visit manipalmap.vercel.app in any browser. The MUJ campus map loads instantly — no app install needed.

STEP 02

Search a destination

Type any building, block, or facility — "Library", "Hostel B", "Mess Hall" — in the smart search bar.

STEP 03

Get your route

Tap a location pin and request walking directions. ORS calculates and renders your route in real time.

STEP 04

Navigate campus

Follow the highlighted path across MUJ. Works on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop.
