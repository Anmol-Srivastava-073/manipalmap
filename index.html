About the Project

Built for every student on campus.

Finding your way around a large university campus can be disorienting, especially for first-year students and guests. Manipal UniNav was built to solve that — a lightweight, open-source navigation platform that runs fast and scales with the campus.

Using maps sourced from OpenStreetMap and routing via the OpenRouteService API, UniNav delivers walking directions and seamless navigation without any app download required.

Module 01
Academic Campus
Navigate to lecture halls, laboratories, libraries, and administrative blocks. Routes optimized for pedestrian pathways across the main campus.
Module 02
Hostel Campus
Explore student housing zones, mess facilities, and nearby recreational spots. Separated routing for the residential area.
Technology
Open Source Stack
OpenStreetMap data, OpenRouteService for pathfinding, deployed on Vercel — entirely free, fast, and community-driven.

Guide

How UniNav works.

01
Open the Website
Go to manipalmap.vercel.app in your browser. The homepage loads with the full interactive MUJ campus map ready to use.
02
Search a Location
Type a building, block, or facility name into the search bar — Library, Hostel, Mess, Admin Block, and more.
03
Get Directions
Select your destination and tap Get Directions. The map draws a walking route optimized for campus paths.
04
Navigate the Campus
Follow the highlighted route to reach your destination. Switch between Academic and Hostel campus zones as needed.
