What is UniNav

Navigation built
for campus life

Finding your way around a large university campus can be overwhelming — especially for new students or visitors. Manipal UniNav is an interactive navigation platform designed to make this effortless.

Built on OpenStreetMap and powered by the OpenRouteService API, UniNav provides real-time walking directions and accurate pathfinding across the entire MUJ campus — free, fast, and open-source.

We split the campus into two navigation zones so routes stay organized and easy to follow, whether you're heading to a lecture or the mess hall.

🏛

College Campus

Academic blocks, libraries, labs, admin buildings, and all academic facilities across the main campus.

🏠

Hostel Campus

Student hostels, mess areas, recreational spots, and all residential zone destinations.

🔗

Open Source

Both map modules are open source — lightweight, scalable, and free to access for everyone on campus.

Getting started

How UniNav works

Step 01
🌐

Open the Website

Visit manipalmap.vercel.app in any browser. The interactive MUJ campus map loads instantly.

Step 02
🔍

Search a Location

Type a building, block, or facility name — like "Library", "Hostel B", or "Mess" — in the search bar.

Step 03
📍

Get Directions

Tap the location pin to view details and request walking directions. The route lights up on the map.

Step 04
🚶

Navigate

Follow the highlighted path to reach your destination anywhere across the MUJ campus.
